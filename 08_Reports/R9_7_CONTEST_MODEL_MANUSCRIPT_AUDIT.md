## Preliminary cross-check calculations, not finalized baseline
Using 2022 full-city population for a reference-system per-capita normalization gives 225793 private small/micro passenger vehicles per million and 45.049 million operator bus-km per million. If 49.9 km is active-day distance and the central-city 46.1% dispatch rate is transferable, the illustrative annual per-car VKT is 8396.42 and private VKT is 1.896 billion km per million. These conditional values demonstrate the sensitivity of activity estimation; they do not establish a matched urban-boundary baseline. A spatial/transfer factor must be stated and tested, not fitted to a desired output.
The R6 award-paper audit reports 1,046,500 motor vehicles including 1,161 buses, and 21,545,123.478 kg CO2 under equal 100 km per vehicle. The implied fleet mean is 205.88 g CO2/km under that paper's assumptions. Current retrospective R7 uses approx 233,175 scaled private cars plus April snapshot buses per million (different vehicle scope), and 167.19 g CO2/km weighted by annual VKT (unclassified bus emissions excluded). The arithmetic is reproducible but the latter is not an equal-100-km or identical energy boundary comparator. Do not claim agreement or validation. Build equal-distance comparison with matching categories, then compare annual VKT only if a justified annual mileage exists. Report the reference discrepancy rather than tuning parameters to an award paper.
