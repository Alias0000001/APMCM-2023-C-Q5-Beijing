# R9.7D — Passenger service and mileage denominator
2022 operator data: 984 million VKT and 1.726 billion boardings published July 2023; mixed propulsion public electric bus category. A 2020 paper (DOI 10.16037/j.1007-869x.2020.08.001, Table 1) reports 10.9 km average central-city bus trip distance. Spatial scope and boarding-leg distance definition are not matched; this is a conditional transfer, not observed PKM. The 2023 100,000-person AFC study combines bus and rail trip chains and provides no matching bus-leg mean. Actual PKM remains NA. Historical private trip occupancy 1.24 (2014 survey, 2016 article) is also only a conditional proxy; no post-cutoff empirical input is used.

PKM=scaled boardings times assumed boarding-leg route distance. At 5/10.9/15 km, bus PKM = 395.092/861.301/1185.277 million/year, effective loads = 8.770/19.119/26.311 persons. These are designed stress cases, not confidence limits. Private occupancy stress 1.0/1.24/1.6. BEV relative occupancy stress 0.81/1.2 for cars and 0.8/1.2 for buses; all class effective occupancies are VKT weighted and at least one. Fixed-VKT primary scenario remains unchanged. Equal-service sensitivity fixes each class's S0 PKM: VKT multiplier=occupancy_S0/occupancy_s; scale that class's WTW and production burden linearly. This does not model modal shift, capacity or congestion. Thirty cases; independent arithmetic checks for conservation and GWP reconciliation passed before commit. Partial GWP still excludes maintenance/EOL/infrastructure/non-exhaust.

Private mileage denominator unresolved: base 49.9*365*0.461=8396.4235 km/car/year versus alternative 49.9*365=18213.5. Alternative private VKT and class burdens scaled once by 1/0.461, bus unchanged. Neither interpretation is confirmed observed mileage. No duplicate migration multiplier. Six-scenario sensitivity is separate, not a statistical interval.
